    📖 How to Bet — Anvil Roulette

    European single-zero roulette: 37 equally likely results (0–36). Select a chip, then click the relevant betting area. Payouts below are net odds; your original stake is also returned on a win.

    🎯 Straight-up · 35:1

    Click the centre of any single number, including 0. Covers 1 number. Hit chance: 1/37 (2.70%).

    ↔ Split · 17:1

    Click the line shared by two adjacent number squares. Covers 2 numbers. Hit chance: 2/37 (5.41%).

    ▦ Street · 11:1

    Click the bottom edge of a three-number column (e.g. 1–2–3). Covers 3 numbers. Hit chance: 3/37 (8.11%).

    ✣ Corner · 8:1

    Click where four number squares meet. Covers 4 numbers. Hit chance: 4/37 (10.81%).

    ➕ Six-line · 5:1

    Covers two adjacent streets, 6 numbers. Hit chance: 6/37 (16.22%). Click the junction on the bottom border between two neighbouring three-number streets to place a six-line bet.

    12 Dozen / Column · 2:1

    Click a dozen below the grid, or a 2-to-1 column box at the end of a number row. Covers 12 numbers. Hit chance: 12/37 (32.43%).

    🔴 Red / Black · 1:1

    Click red or black below the grid. Each covers 18 numbers. Hit chance: 18/37 (48.65%).

    Even / Odd / Low / High · 1:1

    Click the corresponding box. Low means 1–18; High means 19–36. Each covers 18 numbers, 18/37 (48.65%).

    Example: A 1,000-credit street bet on 1–2–3 returns 12,000 credits total if 1, 2 or 3 lands (11,000 profit + 1,000 original stake). Zero loses on all standard even-money bets.
